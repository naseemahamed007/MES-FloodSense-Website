How to interpret this forecast

Forecasts change as new model runs become available. Rainfall probability is not the same as rainfall quantity, and neither one alone determines whether flooding will occur.

What is river discharge?

The volume of water passing a point in a river per unit time, normally measured in cubic metres per second (m³/s).

Preparedness principles

Practical actions before, during and after flooding.

📢

Before

Know evacuation routes, follow official forecasts, charge phones, prepare essential supplies and plan for family members who may need help.

🚫

During

Follow evacuation orders, avoid floodwater, stay away from downed power lines and never use a map or weather dashboard as a substitute for official instructions.

🏠

After

Return only when authorities say it is safe. Avoid contaminated water and damaged electrical equipment, and report hazards to the appropriate authorities.

Data quality notes

Understand the difference between model output and real-world observations.

What this dashboard can do

Display public weather forecasts, visualize rainfall, retrieve modeled river discharge where available, compare values over time, and export the displayed data for educational analysis.

What it cannot verify

It does not independently measure local water levels, inspect drainage infrastructure, map current inundation, confirm emergency alerts or calculate a validated street-level flood probability.
