04 / WEATHER EDUCATION

What should we observe?

Understand the factors that can influence flood risk.

RAINFALL

Intensity and duration

Heavy or prolonged rain can overwhelm drainage systems and increase runoff, particularly in low-lying areas.

WEATHER

Changing conditions

Monitor official weather alerts and forecasts, especially when severe rain is expected.

ENVIRONMENT

Local surroundings

Drainage capacity, rivers, reservoirs, terrain and land use all affect how flooding develops.

06 / COMMUNITY PREPAREDNESS

Flood safety essentials

Simple precautions to help protect people during flooding.

📢

Follow official alerts

Listen to local authorities and India's official weather and disaster-management advisories.

🚫

Avoid floodwater

Do not walk or drive through floodwater. It may be deeper or faster-moving than it appears.

🎒

Prepare essentials

Keep drinking water, essential medicines, a torch, a charged phone and important contacts accessible.

⚡

Stay clear of hazards

Avoid fallen power lines and electrical equipment exposed to water. Report hazards to authorities.

🏠

Know your safe route

Know the local evacuation route and follow official instructions to move to safer ground.

🤝

Help your community

Check on vulnerable neighbours when safe, and contact emergency services when assistance is needed.

For India, use official alerts from the India Meteorological Department and local disaster-management authorities. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
